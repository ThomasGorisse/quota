Quota shows how much of your Claude and Codex usage limits is left, as a ring on your home
screen. One glance, no app to open.

You run the collector on your own machine. It reads the usage figures that Claude Code and
the Codex CLI already write locally, and serves them to this app over your own network. The
app pairs with it once, by scanning a QR code, and then just displays what the collector
reports.

WHAT YOU SEE

• A ring per provider, showing what is REMAINING, not what is spent — the number you
  actually want is the one that is left.
• The time the limit resets, in plain words: "resets in 2 h 10".
• Colour that means one thing everywhere: green while there is room, amber when the window
  is getting tight, red when it is nearly gone, grey when the data is stale.
• Home screen widgets in two sizes, so the answer is on your wallpaper.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Quota is not useful on its own. It is the display for a collector you run yourself, so you
need a machine on your network running it. The pairing screen explains the setup, and the
project README covers it in full.

NO ACCOUNT, NOTHING COLLECTED

• No sign-up, no account, no email.
• The app talks to your collector and to nothing else. There is no server of ours.
• No analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting SDK, no third-party tracker.
• The pairing secret is held in Android's encrypted storage on your device, and never
  leaves it.
• Nothing about your usage, your prompts or your projects is transmitted anywhere. We could
  not read your figures if we wanted to; they never reach us.

Quota is free, open source, and has no in-app purchases. Source, collector and setup:
