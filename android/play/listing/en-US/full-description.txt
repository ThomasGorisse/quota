Quota shows how much of your Claude and Codex usage limits is left, as a ring on your home
screen. One glance, no app to open.

You run the collector on your own machine. It reads the usage figures that Claude Code and
the Codex CLI already write locally, works out a percentage, and sends that percentage to a
small relay at quota.sceneview.dev. This app pairs with the relay once, by scanning a QR
code, and reads your figures back using that pairing token. Only percentages and reset times
travel: no prompts, no file contents, no token counts, no account identifiers.

WHAT YOU SEE

• A ring per provider. By default it shows what is REMAINING — the number you usually want
  is the one that is left — but you can switch it to show what is USED instead. The choice
  is made once and syncs everywhere: phone, widgets and web all follow it.
• The time the limit resets, in plain words: "resets in 2 h 10".
• Colour that means one thing everywhere: green while there is room, amber when the window
  is getting tight, red when it is nearly gone, grey when the data is stale.
• Home screen widgets in two sizes, so the answer is on your wallpaper.

WHAT IT NEEDS

Quota is not useful on its own. It is the display for a collector you run yourself, so you
need a machine running it. The pairing screen explains the setup, and the project README
covers it in full.

NO ACCOUNT, AND ALMOST NOTHING LEAVES YOUR MACHINE

• No sign-up, no account, no email address.
• What the relay holds is a percentage per provider and a reset time, against a random
  pairing identifier. That is the whole payload.
• What it never receives: your prompts, your code, your projects, your file names, your
  token counts, your Claude or OpenAI credentials, and anything identifying you or your
  machine. The collector does not read them and the app could not display them.
• No analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting SDK, no third-party tracker.
• The pairing token is held in Android's encrypted storage on your device.
• Unpair at any time and the relay drops the data.

Quota is free, open source, and has no in-app purchases. If you would rather nothing left
your machine at all, the collector and relay are both in the repository and you can point
the app at your own.
