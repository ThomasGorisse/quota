Your limits.
On the screens you use.

Quota reads the AI accounts you already use on your Mac. A small collector keeps your other screens up to date.

1. Connect your AI tools

On your Mac, sign in to Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. Quota only shows providers it can read. You do not need all three.

You’ll need macOS and Python 3. Check Python in Terminal with python3 --version.

2. Install the collector

Download the Mac collector ↓

Unzip the download, open Terminal in the folder containing the extracted collector folder, then run:

bash collector/install.sh

The installer adds a background task that refreshes every three minutes. macOS may ask you to allow access to Claude Code’s credentials in Keychain.

3. Pair your screens

Open the pairing link printed by the installer, or find it in ~/.quota/pairing.txt. On another device, paste the complete link into Quota. Keep this link private: anyone with it can read your limits.

  • Phone or tablet: open Quota in a compatible browser, or paste the link into the Quota app.
  • Second monitor or an older Android device: open the same link in a modern browser. Use “Full screen” and “Keep awake” where the browser supports them. Leave the device connected to power for a permanent desk display.
  • TRMNL OG: create a private Webhook plugin and paste the Quota template into its Full view. Physical device delivery still needs to be verified on your display.
  • Echo Show: the dashboard may work in Silk; it has not been verified on the physical device.
  • Nest Hub: it has no general-purpose browser. Casting is not implemented, so Quota does not currently support this display.

Pause or remove the collector

Keep the extracted folder in a permanent location before installing: the background task runs the collector from there. To stop collection, run this from the same parent folder:

bash collector/install.sh --uninstall

Your local pairing settings stay in ~/.quota so you can reconnect later. Disconnecting the phone only removes that phone’s pairing; it does not stop the Mac collector.

If a reading stops updating

Wake your Mac and check that the corresponding AI tool is still signed in. Quota keeps the last reading and marks it out of date after ten minutes. Providers that are not connected, including Gemini, are not shown.

What leaves your Mac?

Quota sends usage percentages, reset times, provider and plan labels, and a random pairing token to its Cloudflare relay. Provider credentials are used only to contact the corresponding provider; they are never sent to the relay. Read the privacy policy for storage and retention details.
