Quota — Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Quota shows how much AI usage capacity you have left. It is a private tool: it runs against a backend that you host yourself, and it has no shared account system, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

Quota stores only your own quota numbers — the usage percentages and reset times reported by the AI providers you use — together with a pairing token. Nothing else is collected.

Where it is stored

Your quota numbers are sent to, and stored on, your own Cloudflare Worker — the backend you deploy and control. The developer of Quota operates no server that receives your data, and has no access to it.

The pairing token

The pairing token is a random value generated on your own machine. It identifies a device to your backend so that your screens show your numbers and not someone else's. It is sent only to your own backend. Treat it as a credential and do not share it.

Provider credentials never leave your machine

The credentials used to read your AI provider usage stay on the computer where the collector runs. They are never transmitted to the backend, to the app, or to anyone else. Only percentages and reset times are ever sent.

What Quota does not do

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files or messages are accessed.
  • No accounts are created and no personal profile is built.

Deleting your data

Because the data lives on your own backend, you delete it by clearing your Worker's storage or by taking the Worker down. Uninstalling the app removes the pairing token from the device.

Children

Quota is a developer utility and is not directed at children.
