Know your AI capacity before you start.

Quota brings Claude, Codex and Gemini usage limits together in a calm, clear dashboard. One ring per provider highlights the limit closest to empty, with the window name and reset time beside it. Other limits stay one tap or glance away.

YOUR CAPACITY, CLEARLY
• Switch between capacity left and usage spent. Your preference syncs across your connected screens.
• See session, weekly, reserve and model limits when your provider reports them.
• Read reset times in plain words.
• Recognize old readings: values turn grey when they are out of date, even if another provider has refreshed.
• Try sample data before setting anything up. No sign-in is needed to explore.

ON YOUR APPLE DEVICES
Home screen and lock screen widgets show your most constrained limit. Apple Watch and its complications keep the same reading close at hand. Pair on your iPhone to connect your watch.

CONNECT YOUR MAC
For live readings, install the free Quota collector on the Mac where you use Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. It uses your existing provider sign-in to request usage limits from that provider. Pair the app by pasting the pairing link from your Mac. Keep the Mac online for updated readings; background widget refresh is scheduled by the operating system.

Setup and collector download: https://quota.sceneview.dev/setup.html

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
No ads, analytics or tracking. Your provider credentials stay on your Mac and are used only to authenticate with the respective provider. The relay receives usage percentages, reset times, plan labels and a random pairing identifier, never prompts, source code or provider credentials. You can use the hosted relay or configure your own.

Disconnecting removes the pairing from this device. Hosted snapshots expire seven days after the last collection; history expires after 24 hours. To stop collection, uninstall the Mac collector using the setup instructions.

Quota is free and has no in-app purchases. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Provider availability depends on your account and the data each provider exposes.
